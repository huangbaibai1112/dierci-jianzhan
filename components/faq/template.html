Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear, direct answers about joining and what to expect.

Is registration free to start?

Yes. You can complete the comprehensive personality questionnaire, build your personal profile, and receive compatible member introductions at zero initial cost. If you decide to activate full messaging features, subscription tiers are clearly outlined with no hidden charges.

Who is this platform designed for?

This platform is created exclusively for single adults aged 50 and above. For those who are divorced, widowed, or simply looking for companionship in this chapter of life, members share similar milestones and expectations.

How does the compatibility matching algorithm work?

Upon joining, you complete an in-depth personality questionnaire evaluating five core dimensions: conscientiousness, agreeableness, openness, extraversion, and neuroticism. The platform introduces you to singles whose life goals and communication patterns align with yours.

Will my real identity or address be publicly visible?

Never. Your personal contact details, street address, and real phone number remain private. You communicate through the secure on-platform messaging system until you feel fully comfortable sharing external contact info.

What if I haven’t dated in many years?

Most of our members are re-entering the dating scene after long marriages or extended singlehood. The platform moves at an intentional, unhurried pace, eliminating the pressure and noise of rapid swipe apps.
