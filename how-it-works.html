MEMBER DEFENSE

The Four Golden Rules of Mature Online Dating

While our human care specialists verify accounts 24/7, keeping these four principles in mind ensures complete security.

01

Never Transfer Money or Financial Info

Sincere members will never request wire transfers, gift cards, or financial assistance. Report any member who mentions financial distress immediately.

02

Keep Voice Calls Inside the App

Use the in-app Call Shield for all voice and video calls. Never share personal mobile numbers or home addresses until you have met in person multiple times.

03

Always Meet During Daylight Hours

First encounters belong in well-lit public venues: botanical cafes, museum restaurants, or local coffee shops. Never meet in private residences or secluded parks.

04

Maintain Independent Transportation

Drive yourself or take public transit to and from your meetup venue. Maintaining your own transportation ensures complete control over when you depart.
