Is registration free to start?

Yes. Anyone aged 50 and above can create an account, complete their profile, upload photos, and receive curated Daily Matches at zero initial cost. Who can join this community?

Registration is reserved exclusively for adults aged 50 and older. For divorced, widowed, or single adults wanting sincere companionship, our community connects mature peers who appreciate dignified conversation and shared life milestones.

How do member discovery and daily recommendations work?

Rather than overwhelming you with endless catalogs, you receive handpicked Daily Matches based on your chosen age bracket (50–99), regional radius, and lifestyle preferences. You can also explore the Gallery, refine custom filters, send friendly Winks, or save favorite profiles to revisit whenever you wish.

How is my personal phone number and identity protected?

Your privacy comes first. Built-in video and voice chat keeps your personal telephone number completely private during conversations. You also enjoy full control over profile visibility in discovery, country and region visibility settings, and live verification checks.

How do 1-on-1 meetups work when we decide to meet in person?

When you feel comfortable taking the conversation forward, members can send or receive 1-on-1 Meetup requests for casual daytime coffee or community activities at an unhurried, comfortable pace. Clear public venue guidelines and community safety standards ensure every first meetup takes place in comfortable daylight surroundings.
